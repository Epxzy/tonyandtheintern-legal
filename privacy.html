Privacy Policy: Tony & the Intern

Effective date: 2026-10-30
Last updated: 2026-10-30

This policy explains, in plain language, what personal information Tony & the Intern ("we", "us") collects, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how you can control it. It covers our TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts (@tonyandtheintern), the Discord server Tony And Intern HQ and its bot The Intern.

It is written to meet Canadian federal and provincial privacy laws and Discord's Developer Terms and Policy, and it gives everyone, wherever they live, the rights in section 8. Where the law where you live gives you more rights, those apply too.

Version française : voir POLITIQUE_CONFIDENTIALITE.md.

1. Short version

  • We collect as little as we can: mostly your Discord username, user ID, roles and the messages you post in our server.
  • The bot reads messages in our server to work (give roles, run challenges, moderate). It does not keep a copy of your messages, except moderation logs.
  • The bot only sends a message to an AI when you ask it something yourself, and only if you've turned that feature on.
  • We never sell your information, never use it for advertising, and never use your Discord messages to train AI models.
  • You can ask to see, fix, or delete your information at any time.

2. Person in charge of protecting your information

The person in charge of the protection of personal information is the owner of Tony & the Intern. You can reach them without joining anything:

  • send a direct message to @tonyandtheintern on TikTok, Instagram or Facebook; or
  • in the Discord server Tony And Intern HQ, open a private ticket in #support (only you and the moderators can see it), or message a moderator.

Please don't post private details in a public channel. Moderators who receive a privacy request pass it to the owner and don't keep a copy.

4. Consent and your choices

When you join, the server's entry screen links to this policy before the bot stores anything about you.

What doesn't need your consent: using the information in section 3 to run the server and keep it safe. You can still object at any time (section 8).

What needs your separate consent, each asked on its own, with its own button:

  • AI replies: sending your messages to our AI provider (section 3). Off until you turn it on, and only for members 18 or older.
  • Featuring you: showing your build, name, username or messages in a video or post.

Saying no to either doesn't affect your membership, and you can change your mind at any time.

For people in the EU/UK, our legal bases are: legitimate interests (running and moderating the server, improving the channel), consent (AI replies, featuring you), and legal obligations (for example answering a lawful request).

Children: you must be 13 or older to join. If you are under 14, a parent or guardian must agree before you join; the entry screen asks you to confirm this, and until you do, the bot stores nothing about you except what's needed to show that screen. If we learn that someone under 13, or under 14 without a parent's agreement, has joined, we remove them and delete the bot's records about them within 30 days. Members under 18 can't turn on AI replies.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with services we need to run the channel, and only what they need:

  • Discord (USA) hosts the server and everything posted in it. Discord Privacy Policy
  • TikTok and Meta (Instagram, Facebook) host our content and your comments and messages. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Anthropic (USA) provides the AI model behind the bot's opt-in replies. We also use Anthropic's Claude as an assistant to help run the channel, so it may process the comments, messages and requests you send us, and the bot's records, when helping us answer, moderate or maintain the bot. Anthropic acts as our service provider and, under its commercial terms, does not use this content to train its models. Anthropic Privacy
  • Carl-bot (carl.gg) is our moderation bot. When a moderator warns, mutes, kicks or bans someone, Carl-bot records the case (your user ID and username, the action, the reason, the moderator and the date) on its own servers.
  • Ticket Tool (tickettool.xyz) runs the private tickets in #support. It handles your ticket messages, username and user ID while the ticket is open. If a transcript of a closed ticket is saved, it is kept either on Ticket Tool's servers or in a private area of our server only the owner can see; either way it is deleted on the schedule in section 7.
  • Hosting for the bot (a private computer run by the owner, or a cloud host if we move it) and for these policy pages (GitHub Pages, USA; like any web host, GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, and we don't receive them).
  • Any new service we add will be named here before we use it.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information, and never give it to advertisers or data brokers.

Information stored outside Canada

Discord, Meta, Anthropic and GitHub keep data in the United States; Ticket Tool and Carl-bot may keep data outside Canada; TikTok may keep and access data in several countries where its group operates, as its privacy policy explains. Before sending information to them, we did a written privacy assessment of each, considering how sensitive the information is, why we send it, the provider's contractual and security commitments, and the laws of those countries. We rely on each provider's published data-protection terms as the written agreement. The laws of those countries may let their authorities access the data.

6. Discord and the bot: specific promises

In line with Discord's Developer Terms and Policy:

  • The bot only asks Discord for the permissions it needs to do its jobs.
  • We use Discord data only to run our server, never to sell, advertise, or build profiles of people.
  • We do not use Discord data (including messages) to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • If you ask, we correct or delete the data the bot stored about you within 30 days. If we stop running the bot, or Discord asks us to, its stored data is deleted within 30 days.
  • The bot never sends you a direct message unless you ask it to, and never contacts you outside Discord.
  • We never knowingly collect data from anyone under 13, or sensitive information like health or financial details.
  • To report a problem with the bot, use #support.
  • Messages you post in the server are stored by Discord, not by us. You can delete your own messages in Discord at any time.

8. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • Access: tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • Correct anything wrong or incomplete;
  • Delete it, or stop using it, except what we must keep for an active ban, a legal claim, or to show we answered your request (section 7);
  • Withdraw consent to AI replies or to being featured;
  • Get a copy of what you gave us in a structured, common file format, or have it sent to a person or body the law authorizes to collect it;
  • Stop it from being spread or indexed, where that breaks the law or a court order.

People in the EU/UK can also object to uses based on legitimate interests and restrict processing.

How: use any contact in section 2. We may ask you to confirm you own the account (for example by sending the request from it). It's free. We reply in writing within 30 days of receiving your request; if we don't reply in time, the law treats it as a refusal. If we refuse all or part of a request, we'll tell you why, which provision of law we rely on, and that you have 30 days from the refusal (or from the missed deadline) to ask the privacy authority that applies (section 10) to review it.

Automatic decisions: when the bot takes an automatic action against you (for example deleting a message or muting you), it tells you right away, in the channel or in a private reply, that the action was automatic. On request, we tell you what information was used, the reasons and main factors behind it, and that you can have that information corrected. You can explain your side to a human moderator, who can reverse it.

9. Security

Only the owner can access the bot's stored data and its token. Moderators see moderation actions in Discord, but not the bot's database. Our AI assistant may read the data when helping maintain the bot (section 5). The bot's stored data is encrypted, our accounts use strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and we keep only what we need. No system is perfectly secure.

If a breach happens that could seriously harm you, we'll notify you and the privacy authorities that apply as the law requires, notify Discord as its developer terms require, and record it in our register of incidents.

10. Complaints

Please contact us first (section 2); we want to fix it. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to:

  • Canada: the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or your provincial privacy commissioner
  • EU/UK: your local data protection authority (for the UK, the ICO)

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, post a notice in the Discord announcements channel at least 30 days before they apply. If a change means using your information for a new purpose, we'll ask for your consent first.
